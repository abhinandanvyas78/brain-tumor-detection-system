About Our Project

Advanced AI-powered system for detecting brain tumors from MRI images using state-of-the-art deep learning models.

Project Overview

This Brain Tumor Detection system leverages cutting-edge deep learning technology to analyze MRI brain scans and detect the presence of tumors with high accuracy.

Our system uses multiple pre-trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) including VGG16, ResNet50, MobileNetV2, and custom-built architectures. These models have been trained on a comprehensive dataset of brain MRI images to achieve reliable detection results.

Key Objectives

  • Early Detection: Help medical professionals identify brain tumors at early stages
  • High Accuracy: Achieve reliable predictions with up to 84% accuracy
  • Fast Analysis: Provide instant results for quick medical decision-making
  • Easy to Use: Simple interface for uploading and analyzing MRI images

How It Works

Understanding our AI-powered detection process

1

Image Preprocessing

The uploaded MRI image is preprocessed by resizing to 224x224 pixels, normalizing pixel values, and converting to RGB format for model compatibility.

2

AI Analysis

The preprocessed image is fed into the selected deep learning model, which analyzes patterns and features to detect tumor presence.

3

Result Generation

The model outputs a prediction with confidence score, which is then visualized and presented in an easy-to-understand format.

Dataset Information

Our models are trained on a carefully curated dataset of brain MRI images from Kaggle.

  • Total Images: 253 MRI scans
  • Training Set: 161 images (64%)
  • Validation Set: 41 images (16%)
  • Test Set: 51 images (20%)
  • Image Size: 224x224 pixels
